Data Harmonization Workflow

Data can be dropped into the Files panel, imported using a customized assay design, and surfaced in whatever tabular formats are most useful.

  • Submitted data is shown in two tables: Cell Culture Runs and Cell Culture Results.
  • In the Files panel (right), contributors can drop files for analysis.
  • Continue to the QC tab to see quality control rules applied to the data.
